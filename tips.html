Travel Tips

Everything you need to know before visiting Antique

Before You Go

Plan Your Trip to Antique

Antique is a rewarding destination for travelers who come prepared. Whether you're a first-time visitor or a returning explorer, these tips will help you make the most of your experience in this hidden paradise.

Essential Travel Tips

✈️
Getting There

The nearest airport is Iloilo International Airport. From Iloilo City, take a bus or van going to San Jose de Buenavista (approx. 3–4 hours). Air-conditioned buses and vans are available daily from the terminal near SM Iloilo.

🗓️
Best Time to Visit

The ideal months to visit Antique are November to May (dry season). December is especially festive with the Binirayan Festival. Avoid peak typhoon months of July–October if possible, as some roads and access points may be affected.

🏨
Accommodation

San Jose de Buenavista has the most lodging options, from budget guesthouses to mid-range hotels. For eco-tourism experiences, Tibiao and Pandan offer bamboo cottages and eco-lodges close to the natural attractions.

🚐
Getting Around

Habal-habal (motorcycle taxis) are the most common way to reach remote destinations. Jeepneys and multicabs connect major towns. For island trips like Mararison, hire a bangka (outrigger boat) from Culasi port.

💵
Budget Tips

Antique is budget-friendly. Daily expenses can range from ₱800–₱1,500 for accommodation, meals, and transport. Bring enough cash as ATMs are mainly in San Jose and not always available in remote municipalities.

📡
Connectivity

Mobile signal can be weak or absent in remote areas like highland barangays and uninhabited islands. Download offline maps before heading out. Globe and Smart have the best coverage in urban centers like San Jose.

👗
What to Pack

Pack light, breathable clothing for coastal and lowland areas. Bring a light jacket or sweater for highland visits like Aningalan. Waterproof shoes, insect repellent, sunscreen, and a reusable water bottle are essentials.

Safety
Safety Reminders

Check weather updates before island trips, wear life vests during boat rides, follow local guides for river or mountain activities, and avoid swimming in rough seas or strong currents.

🌿
Eco-Tourism Etiquette

Antique's greatest asset is its untouched natural environment. Always follow Leave No Trace principles — dispose of trash properly, avoid damaging coral reefs, and respect local customs and wildlife when exploring.

🤝
Respecting Local Culture

Antiqueños are known for their warm hospitality. Learn a few Kinaray-a words — even "salamat" (thank you) goes a long way. Ask permission before photographing people, and participate in local activities with humility and respect.
