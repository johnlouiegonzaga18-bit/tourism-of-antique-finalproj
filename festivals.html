Festivals & Culture

Experience the vibrant spirit of Antiqueño heritage

Heritage

A Province Alive with Tradition

Antique is home to a deeply rooted culture expressed through colorful festivals, traditional crafts, and the warm hospitality of its people. These celebrations connect the community to their ancestors while welcoming visitors into their world.

Major Festivals

Annual Festival

Binirayan Festival

Celebrated every December, the Binirayan Festival commemorates the historic landing of Malay datus from Borneo on the shores of Antique around the 13th century. It features street dancing, tribal rituals, trade fairs, and colorful cultural performances that bring the province's founding story to life.

December
San Jose de Buenavista
Street Dancing
Annual Festival

Tubi-an Festival

The Tubi-an Festival in Tibiao celebrates the municipality's rich water resources — its rivers, springs, and waterfalls. Highlights include water-themed activities, kawa bath demos, indigenous performances, and thanksgiving rites honoring nature's abundance in the region.

May
Tibiao
Water Themed
Annual Festival

Hambalos Festival

The Hambalos Festival is a colorful celebration held in Pandan, showcasing the beauty and biodiversity of Antique's coastal and highland landscapes. Named after the hambalos plant, it features indigenous dances, local product showcases, and community activities that highlight the Antiqueño way of life.

August
Pandan

Living Culture

Antiqueño Traditions

🧵
Traditional Weaving

Inabel fabric weaving is a centuries-old craft practiced in many Antique households, producing colorful textiles used in clothing and home decor.

🏺
Pottery

Clay pottery traditions passed down through generations reflect the artistic ingenuity and craftsmanship of Antique's highland communities.

🎵
Kinaray-a Language

The native Kinaray-a language is central to Antiqueño identity — rich with folk songs, oral poetry, and storytelling traditions.

⛪
Heritage Churches

Spanish colonial-era churches dot the province, standing as architectural and spiritual landmarks that connect the past to the present.
