About Antique

A province of hidden wonders in Western Visayas

The Province

What is Antique?

Antique is a province located on the western coast of Panay Island in the Western Visayas region of the Philippines. It is bordered by the Sibuyan Sea to the west and surrounded by mountains to the east, giving it a unique mix of coastal and highland landscapes.

The province is composed of 18 municipalities with San Jose de Buenavista as its capital. Known for its unspoiled environment and warm, welcoming people, Antique has remained one of the less commercialized destinations in the Visayas — making it a true hidden paradise.

Whether you're looking for adventure in its rivers, serenity on its beaches, or cultural richness in its festivals, Antique has something for every kind of traveler.

Geography

Antique covers approximately 2,522 km² and sits on the western side of Panay Island. Its terrain ranges from flat coastal areas to rugged highlands, with the Central Panay Mountain Range forming its eastern border. Major rivers include the Sibalom and Malalison rivers.

Culture & People

The people of Antique, known as Antiqueños, speak Kinaray-a as their primary language. They are known for their strong sense of community, vibrant festivals, and traditional crafts passed down through generations — making visitors feel genuinely welcomed.
